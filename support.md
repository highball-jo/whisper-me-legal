## Common Topics

### Account Issues
- **Can't sign in?** Try resetting your password from the sign-in screen, or sign in with Google or Apple.
- **Want to delete your account?** Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account in the app. This permanently deletes all your data.

### Voice Calls
- **Call not connecting?** Make sure you have a stable internet connection and microphone permission is enabled.
- **Can't hear the companion?** Check your device volume and make sure the app has audio permission.

### Companion
- **Companion not remembering things?** The AI memory system extracts facts after each call. It may take a few conversations for the companion to build up context.
- **Want to change your companion's settings?** Tap your companion's profile to edit name, personality, voice, language, and more.
