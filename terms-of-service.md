## 8. Companion Memory

### 8.1 How Memory Works

Your AI companion uses a multi-layer memory system:
- **Conversation memory:** The companion has access to recent messages within a call
- **Profile facts:** After each call, AI extracts and stores facts about you (name, job, interests, preferences, relationships, etc.)
- **Call summaries:** Each call is summarized and stored with semantic embeddings for future retrieval
- **Relationship narrative:** Periodically, the AI generates a narrative summary of your relationship history

### 8.2 Memory Limitations

- Memory extraction is performed by AI and may be inaccurate
- Facts may be incorrectly attributed, misunderstood, or lost
- The companion may reference memories inaccurately or at inappropriate times
- Memory is designed to enhance the experience but is not a reliable record of your conversations

### 8.3 Memory and Third-Party Processing

Memory extraction requires sending your conversation data to xAI (Grok) for processing, and call summaries to OpenAI for embedding generation. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
